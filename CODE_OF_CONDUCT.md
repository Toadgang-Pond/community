# Toadgang Pond Code of Conduct

We welcome people from different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and constructive; critique ideas, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, discriminate against, or dox participants.
- Avoid spam, scams, unsolicited promotions, and misleading security claims.
- Do not post private credentials, recovery phrases, or personal data.
- Keep discussions focused and assume good intent without ignoring harmful behavior.

Maintainers may warn participants, edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, and refer serious threats to appropriate channels. Decisions should be proportionate and consistently applied.

For sensitive safety concerns, avoid posting private details publicly. Maintainers will publish a dedicated contact channel before opening high-volume community moderation.
